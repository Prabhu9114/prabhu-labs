Command Center v5.0

A Telethon automation suite for high-volume Telegram content operations.

The Problem

High-volume content work means juggling many source feeds, converting links, and scheduling posts without tripping Telegram’s limits.

The Solution

Command Center v5.0 connects directly over MTProto and handles multi-source feeds, link conversion and staggered scheduling in one suite, with FloodWait-aware pacing and state mirrored to private log channels.

How It Works

  1. 01

    MTProto Telethon Async

    Direct Telegram client connection that handles continuous feeds, with FloodWait-aware pacing.

  2. 02

    Dynamic Queue Stagger

    Spreads posts across time slots within Telegram’s 100 scheduled-message limit.

  3. 03

    Cloud Log Sync

    Mirrors state and configuration into private Telegram log channels.
